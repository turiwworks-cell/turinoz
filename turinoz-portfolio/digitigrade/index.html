Digitigrade

dig·i·ti·gradeadj.walking on the digits

In zoology, it means walking on the toes. This one walks on numerals. Drawn live by one Luau script inside Rive, it plants a foot on a digit, presses it down, and clicks it like a key. Point anywhere on the page and it comes to you. Leave it alone and it finds its own way.
