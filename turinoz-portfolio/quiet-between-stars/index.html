01 — Brief

A space, rather
than a scene.

Petie wanted his office to feel like the inside of a ship: somewhere to think clearly, away from urgency. Three tall OLED screens, on for hours. So it had to be easy to live with.

The rules

  1. 01Deep black, lots of empty space
  2. 02Distant, quiet structure
  3. 03Depth over particle count
  4. 04Motion slow enough to forget
  5. 05No visible start or end
  6. 06Silent

Petie’s way of putting itIdle is still water.
Hyperspace is a smooth current.

02 — Process

From a test to a world.

Petie set the brief and reviewed the key moments, about 3 or 4 out of 10 by his own count. The calls in between were mine.

  1. 01Test
  2. 02Direction
  3. 03System
  4. 04Weight
  5. 05Hyperspace
  6. 06Refinement
  7. 07Handover

01Test

Show the method first.

Before there was a project, I offered a test: procedural, no outside images, no plugins, a few controls. Rough on purpose, to show how I work.

My call
Start with a system, not a single shot.
Petie
Saw enough in it to plan the full project.

02Direction

Give the black more room.

Petie liked the rhythm, but the clouds were too present. I pulled them back and made four styleframes that differ mostly in density and empty space.

My call
A02 Darker, the one with the most room to breathe.
Petie
Picked the same one. It became the visual baseline.

03System

Build the system before the renders.

A change in the code can reshuffle the randomness, so an early render may never match the final build. I built the system first and rendered Idle after it.

My call
System first, renders after.
Petie
Agreed. He wanted a solid base, not a long list of features.

04Weight

Light enough to live with.

Every star had a real place in 3D space. It looked right but it was heavy, so I kept looking for a lighter build that keeps the depth you can see.

My call
Keep the depth you can see, cut the cost you can’t.
Petie
Chose speed and clarity over a little more physical accuracy.

05Hyperspace

Hyperspace, in its own voice.

My first entry ran 15 seconds, and Petie found it long. I also tried a star pass we had ruled out, and flagged it as a test.

My call
A01’s density with B01’s motion.
Petie
Liked where Entry and Return were going. Asked for twice the speed and a slow turn in the tunnel.

06Refinement

Forward, not around.

Petie’s one major note: the motion circled instead of moving forward. I split Drift X and Y, let Z lead, and let the atmosphere evolve instead of loop.

My call
Turn a feeling into parameters.

The same 25 seconds. The change is quiet.

07Handover

Something easy to live with.

I sent the project and the guide before the big renders, so Petie could learn the system on his own machine.

My call
Design the handoff, not just the file.

“Your guide was great and I felt right at home in the project.”

Petie ClarkClient

His last note: no more changes needed.
