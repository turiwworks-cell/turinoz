01 — The brief

A space, rather
than a scene.

Petie’s brief: he wanted his office to feel like the inside of a ship: somewhere to think clearly, away from urgency. Three tall OLED screens, on for hours. So it had to be easy to live with.

The brief in six rules

  1. 01Deep black, lots of empty space
  2. 02Distant, quiet structure
  3. 03Depth over particle count
  4. 04Motion slow enough to forget
  5. 05No visible start or end
  6. 06Silent

Petie’s way of putting itIdle is still water.
Hyperspace is a smooth current.

02 — Process

Real stars,
one universe.

Petie set the brief and reviewed the key moments, about 3 or 4 out of 10 by his own count. The calls in between were mine.

01Build

Layers and code, not a picture.

After the brief I went into design. I built the piece from layers and expressions in After Effects: 400 real stars, each with code of its own and code they share.

My call
Build a system, so the world can keep changing.
Petie
Agreed. He wanted a solid base, not a long list of features.

02Weight

Real has a cost.

All that code made the renders heavy. Going back to fakes would have changed the look by maybe two or three percent. I stayed real, and cut the cost of the clouds and nebula instead, to make room for the stars.

My call
The premium route: real stars, lighter clouds.

03Universe

Every layer is a real layer, inside one universe.

Stars, clouds, nebula and the hyperspace tunnel each sit in After Effects’ own 3D space, inside one composition: QBS_UNIVERSE. The depth you see is real depth, so the parallax is real too.

All native After Effects.

04Hyperspace

Hyperspace, in its own voice.

My first entry ran 15 seconds, and Petie found it long. I also tried a star pass we had ruled out, and flagged it as a test.

My call
A01’s density with B01’s motion.
Petie
Liked where Entry and Return were going. Asked for twice the speed and a slow turn in the tunnel.

05Handover

Something easy to live with.

I sent the project and the guide before the big renders, so Petie could learn the system on his own machine.

My call
Design the handoff, not just the file.

“Your guide was great and I felt right at home in the project.”

Petie ClarkClient

His last note: no more changes needed.

03 — System

A world you can keep changing.

The films came with the system that makes them. One master seed reshapes the whole scene, and every part keeps its own controls.
