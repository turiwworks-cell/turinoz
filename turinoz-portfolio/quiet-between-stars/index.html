Ambient motion system for Petie Clark

A slow, silent world for Petie’s office, made for three tall OLED screens. I led the creative direction and the design, and built the After Effects system that runs it.

01 — The brief

A space, rather
than a scene.

Petie’s brief: he wanted his office to feel like the inside of a ship: somewhere to think clearly, away from urgency. Three tall OLED screens, on for hours. So it had to be easy to live with.

The brief in six rules

  1. 01Deep black, lots of empty space
  2. 02Distant, quiet structure
  3. 03Depth over particle count
  4. 04Motion slow enough to forget
  5. 05No visible start or end
  6. 06Silent

Petie’s way of putting itIdle is still water.
Hyperspace is a smooth current.

02 — Primary Idle

Still water, in real depth.

  • 120Hero stars
  • 400Micro stars
  • 0Plugins, images, outside assets

Every star is a real shape layer, with After Effects expressions inside that tell it where to sit and how to behave.

Clouds and nebula are merged into a few layers, each at its own depth, so their parallax is real too.

The problem

Real has a cost.

520 star layers and over 1,700 expressions, clouds and nebula placed in real 3D, one camera, all at 6480 × 3840 in 16-bit. Faking it would have changed the look by two or three percent, so I kept it real and made it lighter.

How I solved it

  1. 01No blur effects in Idle; Extract makes the soft edges.
  2. 02Turbulent Noise for clouds, Turbulent Displace for nebula.
  3. 03One 3D solid per cloud, one feathered mask on its edge.
  4. 042 px stars, no effects; one Glow for all hero stars.
  5. 05No motion blur anywhere.
03 — Hyperspace

A smooth current, on a loop.

  • 1Flat noise map
  • 170Second loop
  • 0Keyframes inside the loop

The tunnel is one flat map of Fractal Noise and VR Fractal Noise, wrapped into a cylinder with CC Cylinder and seen from inside. A larger copy of the same map makes the walls near the lens.

All of its motion is expressions, driven by five controls: Variation, Speed, Depth Falloff, Twirl and Opacity.

The problem

No visible start or end.

It plays for hours on three tall screens, so any jump in the noise, speed or turn would show on every loop. And Petie had to be able to change the speed himself.

How I solved it

  1. 01One 170-second loop holds it all, between the transitions.
  2. 02Speed rounds to whole passes, so the map ends where it began.
  3. 03The noise evolves in whole turns over the loop.
  4. 04Twirl swings 4° each way per loop: Petie’s slow turn.
04 — Transitions

Five seconds each way.

  • 5Seconds each way
  • 42Keyframes, all in the transitions
  • 1Switch

Entry and Return are the only keyframed parts of the engine. Nine sliders on one control layer, KF_TRANSITIONS, move the camera, the lens, the star shine and each layer’s opacity, and soft masks open the tunnel from the center.

One slider, Scene Switch, decides what the scene is. At 0 it is Primary Idle and every key is ignored; at 1 it plays the whole journey.

The problem

Fast, without a cut.

My first entry ran 15 seconds and Petie found it long. In 5 seconds stars, clouds, lens and tunnel all have to change, and both ends must land exactly on the loops.

How I solved it

  1. 01One 180 s timeline: Entry, loop, Return. Each join is one frame.
  2. 02Every key on one control layer, read by expressions.
  3. 03In order: shine, tunnel in, then stars, nebula, clouds out.
  4. 04Return starts the camera far back and eases it home.
05 — Motion system

99,000 scenes. Every one loops.

  • 99,000Scenes from one seed
  • 4States, one loop
  • 1,726Expressions

One slider, Master Variation, seeds the hero stars, micro stars, cloud, nebula and tunnel. Any value from 0 to 99,000 makes a new scene.

Every scene runs the same journey: Idle, Entry, Hyperspace, Return, and back to Idle on the frame it left.

The problem

Any seed, no seams.

Petie could pick any seed or move any slider. Every result still had to loop, and still travel to Hyperspace and back without a cut.

How I solved it

  1. 01Seeds change shapes and places, never timing.
  2. 02Everything moves in whole cycles of its loop.
  3. 03Stars stay put. The camera drifts and comes home.
  4. 04Transitions are keys on one layer, for every seed.
