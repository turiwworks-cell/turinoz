The brief

A space,
rather than a scene.

Petie wanted his office to feel like the inside of a ship: somewhere to work quietly, think clearly, and feel removed from urgency. The future installation was three large vertical OLED displays, with ultrawide versions for his desk.

The work had to stay easy to live with for hours. Deep black, distant structure, and motion slow enough to forget it was there. That became the rule for everything that followed.

04 — Refinement

Drifting forward.
Less going around.

Petie’s first review caught a swirl in the idle. I separated X and Y drift, gave Z movement the lead, and replaced the atmosphere’s looping offset with evolution.

The change is quiet. Both excerpts show the same 25 seconds at normal speed; the aim was a steadier sense of direction, while keeping the scene alive.

Practical at full size

Easy to change.
Reasonable to render.

I kept refining the build after the look was working. In my production tests, a full render went from around 7½ hours to 40–50 minutes. Petie later reported about 25 minutes for his full package.

Times reported during production on different machines; they are observations, not a controlled benchmark.
