scoreline

A football app for match nights. Three bars cut from five spectra, set in Hanken Grotesk.

Mark · Lockup · Color · Type · Motion · In use · Misuse · KitDesign by Turinoz

The whole brand on one sheet. The chapters below explain each part.

01 / Mark

Three bars on a 12-unit grid.

Each bar is 3 units wide. The sides are 6 tall, the middle 11. The left bar drops 3 units, the right one 2, so the mark never sits level.

Construction
Clear spaceOne bar width on every side.

Nothing enters the 3-unit border around the mark: no text, no edge, no other logo.

BarsLeft Dusk, middle Violet, right Floodlight.

The order never changes. Each bar keeps its own spectrum, top to bottom.

Minimum size16 px tall on screen, 6 mm in print.
02 / Lockup

The mark leads, the name follows.

One horizontal lockup. The wordmark is Hanken Grotesk Medium in lowercase, outlined. The left bar sits on its baseline.

Primary · on night
On white
One color · on the field
One color · chalk on surface
SpacingGap between mark and name: 1⅔ bar widths.

Clear space is one bar width, the same as the mark on its own.

One colorWhen the ground fights the spectra, fade.

On the field gradient or in a single ink, each bar fades in one color: sides fade up, the middle fades down.

03 / Color

Five spectra on a night ground.

The app lives at night. Black carries the screen, chalk carries the text, and the spectra arrive only where something matters. Names are taken from the pitch.

05 / Motion

The mark moves like a score changing.

Sides go up and come back from below, the middle goes down and comes back from above. Two moves, nothing else.

Roll

The color rolls inside each bar. For loading and for moments inside the app.
