A football app for match nights.

v1.0 · October 2026Designed by Turinoz
01

Mark

Three bars, each 3 units wide. The sides are 6 units tall, the middle 11. The left bar drops 3, the right drops 2.

Construction
Clear spaceOne bar width on every side.
BarsDusk, Violet, Floodlight, left to right.

Each keeps its own spectrum, top to bottom.

Minimum size16 px on screen, 6 mm in print.

Lockup

Hanken Grotesk Medium, lowercase, outlined. The baseline meets the foot of the left bar.

Primary
On white
One color, on the field
One color, chalk
SpacingMark to name: 1⅔ bar widths.

Clear space is one bar width, as for the mark alone.

One colorOn the field, or in a single ink.

Each bar fades in one color: the sides fade up, the middle fades down.

Motion

Two moves. The sides go up, the middle goes down, and each comes back in from the other edge.
