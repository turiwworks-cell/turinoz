Buttons

Two buttons, each a color square and a label. On hover the color slides across the label and the square goes dark, so the mark sits on it like the app icon. On “Open Curve Lab” the mark turns a quarter every 2.6 seconds and the square takes the next of three colors, then the loop starts again. “Show me” stays on the interface color: press it and the word fades out, the box eases to the new width, and the next word fades in.
