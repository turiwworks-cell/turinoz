Curve Lab

I lost my graph editor. So I made one.

After years in After Effects, I wanted that same familiar control over motion on the web. Curve Lab is inspired by AE’s graph editor, with its own design and a few extra tools like springs.

A compact tool for finding your numbers.

It never fills the screen. Graph, nine test objects and presets sit together like a little machine on your desk: shape the curve, watch it play at ¼×, ½× or 1×, copy the numbers, get back to work.

03Export

A new file. Your original stays as it was.

Curve Lab never writes into the page you opened. Export saves a copy with -edited in its name, and every change sits in one marked block you can read, keep or delete. This panel follows your edits above.

04How it was made

Made with Claude. Designed and directed by me.

One light HTML file. No install, no account, nothing to set up. Open it and start.
