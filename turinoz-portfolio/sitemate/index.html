01 — Agent states

Every state, on loop.

Each clip is one thing the agent can be doing. Loops close on their own first frame, so they repeat without a seam, and transitions move the agent between its two thinking shapes. Three states also ship a deep-thinking version. Switch them to Deep to compare.

    05 — Early directions

    Before the sketch.

    Quick studies in weight, volume and line for how the agent should look while it thinks. None of them shipped, but they pointed the way, including the idea of switching between a cone and a cylinder.

    These studies spin the opposite way to the final. The direction was reversed when the motion was locked.
